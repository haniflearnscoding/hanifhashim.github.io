Typography

Three typeface families serve distinct roles, balancing retro character with modern readability.

Headlines — Press Start 2P

PIXEL GHOSTS

The Revival of Retro Game Aesthetics

Used for display headings, UI labels, and decorative text. Evokes the bitmap fonts of 8-bit consoles. Best at larger sizes; avoid for body copy.

Body — Source Sans 3

In an era defined by photorealistic rendering engines and 4K displays, a curious counter-movement has emerged: the deliberate embrace of low-resolution pixel art. This is not merely nostalgia — it represents a conscious design language that privileges constraint, legibility, and emotional directness over technical spectacle.

Primary reading typeface. Clean, humanist sans-serif with excellent screen rendering. Weights 300 (light), 400 (regular), 600 (semibold), 700 (bold).
