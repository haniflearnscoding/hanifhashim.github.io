The Concept

Your shift is almost over, but the hospital ward has descended into chaos. Infectious patients roam the halls, and your only protection? The surgical masks scattered throughout the building. Find them, survive, and clock out before your time runs out.

The theme "Mask" inspired us to explore healthcare workers' reality - where masks are the thin line between safety and infection. As a nurse trying to escape an overrun ward, every mask you collect extends your survival. It's a love letter to frontline workers wrapped in retro pixel art horror.

// DEVELOPMENT EVOLUTION

PROTOTYPE 01 // MOVEMENT

Day one: establishing the core. Grid-based movement through tight hospital corridors. The nurse sprite takes shape - navigating the maze of gurneys and medical equipment. Basic collision detection and the foundation of our top-down perspective.

PROTOTYPE 02 // THREAT

Enter the infected patients. They now wander the halls with basic AI pathfinding, coughing and spreading contagion. The tension builds - contact means losing precious health. The mask collection mechanic takes form, creating risk-reward decisions throughout the ward.

PROTOTYPE 03 // POLISH

The final push. UI elements lock into place - health hearts, the ticking clock, mask counter. Sound design adds urgency. The pixel art background transforms empty space into a chaotic hospital ward. Every interaction gets that satisfying "juice."

The 48-Hour Sprint

Friday Night: Find the Fun

Theme reveal hit at 5pm. By midnight, we had our concept locked - a hospital survival game where masks are both protection and resource. Paper prototyping helped us nail the core loop before writing a single line of code.

Saturday: The Crunch

Sprites came to life. The patient AI went through three iterations before feeling threatening but fair. We cut planned features ruthlessly - no inventory system, no multiple wards. Just one tight, replayable experience.

Sunday: Ship It

Polish day. Added screen shake on damage, particle effects for pickups, and that satisfying clock-out animation. Playtested with fresh eyes from other teams. Submitted with an hour to spare.
